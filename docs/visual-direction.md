# CSNoel Visual Direction

The public-facing experience uses a **photo-led healthcare staffing visual system** inspired by the pace and confidence of StaffMed while preserving CSNoel’s own identity. The intended feeling is energetic, organized, and trustworthy rather than clinical or generic. Photography will be prominent, supported by a deep navy field, a confident cobalt accent, warm off-white space, and lively teal highlights.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `RcerzZ4XwWPO.jpg` | Homepage hero | Wide hospital corridor with clinicians in motion; ideal for showing momentum and coordinated care | Unsplash search result; reviewed against Unsplash’s license page, which permits free commercial use and does not require attribution, though credit is appreciated. |
| `hlcI6JlvdtXR.jpg` | Candidate callout and mobile portrait crop | Detail-oriented clinician image with a stethoscope; supports the clinician-focused value proposition | Unsplash search result; subject to the same Unsplash licensing review. |

The hero image will sit inside a rounded, high-contrast visual panel rather than act as a full-bleed background. This preserves readable copy, creates a more crafted composition, and supports responsive crops. The candidate image will be used in a taller editorial card with a dark overlay and a compact opportunity badge. Both images require descriptive alternative text and will be served from project-managed static asset URLs rather than bundled locally.
